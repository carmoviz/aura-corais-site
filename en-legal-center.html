Our commitments to everyone who visits this website

Clear information. We present only projected gross revenue and appreciation scenarios in percentages, always identified as conservative estimates — never as a promise of returns.

Images clearly marked. Every image is illustrative and flagged as such, with private areas indicated as approximate.

Only the data we need. We collect just what is required to answer you: no trackers, no browsing profiles and no data sales.

Official channels and protection against fraud

The only official channels are the e-mail address contato@auracorais.com and the WhatsApp number published on this website.

We never request payments through this website. Amounts are only handled after a signed contract and through the accounts formally indicated by the developer. If you receive a request for a deposit or a reservation fee through any other channel, confirm it with us before transferring anything.

Structure planned for the development

AURA Corais is developed by VRV Incorporadora. The planned structure includes a special purpose entity (SPE), registration of the development with the property registry office, a segregated asset regime and separate accounting.

These items are formalised as the structuring, registration and construction stages progress, and the development is subject to registration.

Requests about personal data

To access, correct, port or delete your data, or to withdraw consent, write to contato@auracorais.com with the subject "LGPD". We reply within 15 days.
