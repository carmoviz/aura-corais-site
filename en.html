    The AURA concept

    An address to live the destination. An asset designed to work.

    Lofts designed for hospitality, arranged around a pool, gardens and shared living areas. There are 30 lofts on two floors and in two layouts: the ground floor, with a private outdoor area, and the upper floor, with a balcony. Architecture and landscaping integrated into the spirit of Arraial d'Ajuda.

    Frequently asked questions

    Questions and answers

    What is AURA Corais?

    Contemporary lofts at Loteamento Corais do Arraial, in Arraial d'Ajuda, Porto Seguro – Bahia, Brazil, between the historic village and the Parracho and Pitinga beaches: 30 lofts in two layouts, arranged around a pool, gardens and a wellness circuit. Developed by VRV Incorporadora.

    What are the layouts and areas?

    Upper loft with 32.42 m² of private area (28.71 m² indoors + a 3.71 m² balcony) and ground loft with 28.71 m² indoors + a private outdoor area (40.58 m² in the typical unit; 39.84 to 72.21 m² depending on the unit). Approximate areas; final measurements as approved in the project.

    What does each loft include?

    Integrated living and sleeping area with a double bed and a sofa bed, compact kitchen with sink, cooktop and mini fridge, private bathroom with a shower box, work counter and wardrobes. The ground loft has a private outdoor area (deck and hot tub contracted separately); the upper loft has a balcony. Part of the décor is contracted separately.

    Which shared areas are planned?

    Reception, pool, restaurant and gourmet area, a gym surrounded by greenery, a wellness circuit with a meditation space and a steam sauna, gardens with Balinese daybeds, a bicycle rack and a laundry. Shared areas as planned in the project and the descriptive memorandum.

    When is the launch?

    Soon. The registration of the development and the other stages follow the planned timeline, and the dates of each stage will be announced after the respective approvals. Sign up to be the first to receive the presentation.

    Is the revenue shown guaranteed?

    No. The figures are projections of gross revenue, before any deduction, estimated from market references. Conservative projection; it is not a promise or a guarantee of results. It depends on the market, on the form of management chosen by the owner and on future conditions.

    Do income and appreciation add up?

    No. Income and appreciation are different in nature and are presented separately; the indicators do not add up. Appreciation appears only as percentage scenarios and is an estimate, not a guarantee; it depends on the market over time.

    Can I use the loft and also rent it out?

    Yes. Your property, your choices: use it during your own stays, put the unit on short-term rentals or combine both. How to manage it is the owner's decision.
